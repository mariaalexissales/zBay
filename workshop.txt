description=[h1]zBay[/h1]
description=[h2]A marketplace for multiplayer servers[/h2]
description=[b]List what you find, buy what you need, bid on what you want - from anywhere, with the currency your server chooses.[/b]
description=
description=Right-click the ground and choose [b]Open zBay[/b], or hover the map icon on the left sidebar and click the zBay cell that flies out with it. No terminal to find, no power needed.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What It Does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Fixed-price listings[/b] - sell one or a stack, buyers take as many as they like
description=[*] [b]Auctions[/b] - opening bid, optional buyout, and a bid in the last minutes extends the clock so nobody is sniped
description=[*] [b]A bank[/b] - deposit your server's currency, pay and get paid from your balance, withdraw it again
description=[*] [b]Mail[/b] - anything too heavy to carry waits for you instead of being lost
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Bids are held, not promised.[/b] A bid leaves your bank when you place it and comes straight back if you are outbid, so nobody wins with money they have since spent.
description=
description=[b]Items come back the way they went in.[/b] Condition, custom names, weapon parts, what is in a bottle, and other mods' own item data all survive a sale. An item zBay cannot rebuild exactly is refused at the counter, not broken in the post.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Your Mailbox[/h2]
description=When you buy something you cannot carry, or win an auction while logged off, it goes to your mail. Right-click any mailbox and choose [b]Set as my zBay mailbox[/b], then right-click it again to collect. Mailboxes can be picked up, so you can take one home.
description=
description=Your mail is kept by the server, not inside the mailbox, so nobody can loot it and nothing is lost if the mailbox is moved or destroyed. Claim another and it is all still there.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]For Server Owners[/h2]
description=Everything is in one readable file, created the first time the world starts:
description=
description=[i]Zomboid\Lua\zBay\config.json[/i]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Currency[/b] - any item, or several with different values. Vanilla money, gold bars, a modded coin, bottle caps
description=[*] [b]Fees and limits[/b] - the market's cut, listings per player, how long a listing lasts
description=[*] [b]Auctions[/b] - on or off, the lengths on offer, the minimum raise, buyouts
description=[*] [b]Blacklist[/b] - items and categories that cannot be sold
description=[*] [b]Mailboxes[/b] - which tiles count, and whether one is needed at all
description=[/list]
description=
description=Edit it, save, and press [b]Reload Settings[/b] in the window. No restart. A mistake in the file never takes the server down - the old settings stay and the console names the line.
description=
description=[b]Built to stay quiet.[/b] The server never broadcasts the market. A client is sent the one page it is looking at and its own balance, and only players with the window open are told when something changes.
description=
description=A readable backup of every balance and listing is kept beside the settings and rewritten every few minutes, for the day a server is killed or rolled back.
description=
description=Want to try a setup before it goes on a server? It all works in singleplayer too.
description=
